Capabilities & Services

Five coordinated lines. One service.

Whatever the situation, Vanguard coordinates the response from a single point of contact. Below is the full picture of what we cover — and what to do when a problem doesn't fit any of it.

Reach the 24/7 team
01

Intelligence, Monitoring & Security

Eyes on the water before anything goes wrong — and the security advisory to keep voyages safe through high-risk areas.

  • 24/7 manned vessel monitoring and maritime domain awareness
  • High-risk-area voyage support and security advisory
  • Counter-piracy coordination and incident response
  • Intelligence-led risk assessment by route and region
  • Ship Security Alert System monitoring and response
02

Crisis & Casualty Management

Holding the many threads of a casualty together — owners, insurers, flag, port and coastal authorities — under one coordinated lead.

  • Multi-agency liaison across owners, insurers, flag state, port and coastal authorities
  • Place-of-refuge coordination
  • Crew evacuation coordination and welfare support
  • P&I club and underwriter liaison and documentation support
  • Casualty reporting and incident close-out
03

Salvage & Emergency Response

The core discipline — making a casualty safe and recovering the situation, including the newer fuel and battery risks the modern fleet brings.

  • Emergency towage and disabled-vessel assistance
  • Refloating and dewatering operations
  • Vessel stabilisation and damage control
  • Marine firefighting coordination
  • Alternative-fuel & new-technology incident response — LNG, methanol, ammonia, lithium-ion battery fires
  • Explosion and fire casualty management
04

Wreck Removal & Special Projects

From harbour clearance to subsea recovery and offshore-wind incidents — the complex, non-standard jobs that need a coordinated network.

  • Wreck removal and harbour clearance
  • Cargo and bunker recovery from casualty vessels
  • Anchor and lost-equipment recovery
  • Subsea inspection and ROV operations
  • Offshore and nearshore special projects
  • Offshore-wind incident response and recovery
05

Pollution & Environmental Response

Containing the environmental side of a casualty and liaising with the authorities who hold the coastline.

  • Oil-spill response coordination and contractor mobilisation
  • Pollution containment, recovery and environmental-authority liaison
  • Hazardous-material and chemical-incident coordination
