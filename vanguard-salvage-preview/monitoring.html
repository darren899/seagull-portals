Proactive Monitoring & Intelligence

We're watching. Not waiting for the call.

Through Blue Nexus, Vanguard provides a proactive monitoring and response framework designed to identify developing maritime risks before they become full-scale incidents — combining 24/7 manned monitoring with vessel tracking, intelligence analysis and operational response planning.

Request a platform walkthrough
01The operations picture

One live picture, watched around the clock.

AIS data, regional intelligence, water-depth information, local-resource availability and situational reporting are brought together to create a live operational picture — so the team can detect emerging concerns, verify the situation, connect the right resources, mobilise support and coordinate through to resolution.

02No handover gap

Monitoring and response are the same team.

What makes this model different is that monitoring and response are not two separate services. They are the same team, working the same picture.

Our intelligence and monitoring staff watch vessel movements, track risk indicators and build situational awareness around the clock. When a situation begins to develop — a vessel deviating from its route, a distress signal, or an unfolding casualty reported by an owner or P&I club — the same team that was monitoring it rolls directly into casualty coordination, without handover delay or information loss. There is no gap between detection and response. That continuity, from proactive watch to active case management, is one of the most practical advantages we offer.

03What it brings together

A structured process — detect, verify, link, mobilise, coordinate.

  • 24/7 manned monitoring and active watchkeeping
  • AIS data combined with intelligence-led analysis
  • Regional resource awareness, water-depth information and local operating insight
  • A structured process to detect, verify, link, mobilise and coordinate response activity
  • Direct connection between monitoring, intelligence, salvage planning and live response coordination
  • AI-assisted detection of distress indicators, with real-time reporting from vessels and partners
04Why it matters

Early detection is an operational advantage — and a commercial one.

Blue Nexus monitors over 20,000 vessels globally, using AI-assisted detection of distress indicators combined with real-time reporting from vessels and partners, and liaison with naval and regional networks. The result is that we identify incidents early, before they escalate.

For owners and insurers, that early detection isn't just operational. It's commercial. The first hours of a casualty define the outcome — and the cost.
