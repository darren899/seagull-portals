01The problem

One casualty. One point of escalation.

When a casualty occurs, owners face a fragmented response — separate salvors, separate environmental responders, separate documentation, separate lines of accountability, each negotiated under pressure.

Vanguard coordinates marine salvage and casualty response as a single-source service: one 24/7 incident desk, one coordinated operating model, and one contracted point of escalation from initial notification through to safe-haven hand-back. The recovery work itself is delivered through vetted salvage, towage and environmental partners, mobilised to the casualty wherever it sits — Vanguard runs the case, the network does the work.

03Response sequence

From notification to hand-back.

Every casualty runs the same disciplined sequence — confirmed against the contractual route at the outset, documented end to end.

01

Notify

24/7 desk receives notification. Scope, location, urgency and contractual route confirmed within the hour.

02

Assess

Salvage master and survey team mobilised to the casualty. Damage, stability and environmental exposure evaluated.

03

Stabilise

Casualty made safe. Environmental hazards contained. Response strategy agreed with owners, P&I and authorities.

04

Recover

Refloating, towage, cargo recovery or wreck removal under Vanguard coordination through vetted partners.

05

Restore

Casualty delivered to safe haven. Environmental sign-off. Documentation handed to owners and underwriters.
