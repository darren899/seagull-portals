01Why Vanguard

Modern coordination for one of the sea's oldest disciplines.

The principles of salvage never change: respond quickly, assess the problem, stabilise the situation, and work toward the safest outcome for vessel, crew, cargo and environment.

What has changed is the operating environment — advanced vessel technology, specialised cargoes, environmental obligations, regulatory scrutiny, and the expectations of owners, insurers and authorities. Vanguard brings experienced maritime professionals together with intelligence-led monitoring, practical response planning and a trusted operational network to coordinate an effective response, wherever it is needed. We hold one line of accountability from first alert through to hand-back; the recovery is delivered by partners we have vetted in the field.

04Response sequence

From first alert to hand-back.

Every casualty runs the same disciplined sequence — confirmed against the right contractual route at the outset, documented end to end.

01

Notify

24/7 response team receives the alert. Scope, location, urgency and contractual route confirmed within the hour.

02

Assess

Salvage masters and survey team mobilised to the casualty. Damage, stability and environmental exposure evaluated.

03

Stabilise

Casualty made safe. Environmental hazards contained. Strategy agreed with owners, P&I and authorities.

04

Recover

Refloating, towage, cargo recovery or wreck removal under Vanguard coordination through vetted partners.

05

Restore

Casualty delivered to safe haven. Environmental sign-off. Documentation handed to owners and underwriters.
