03Statement of qualifications

Experienced personnel, practical coordination, real reach.

Vanguard's salvage and emergency-response capability is built around experienced personnel, practical coordination and access to a wider maritime support network — drawing on backgrounds in vessel operations, salvage engineering, intelligence analysis, naval architecture, maritime security and incident response. We support owners, operators, insurers and other stakeholders across a range of emergency scenarios:

  • Casualty response and incident coordination
  • Emergency towage coordination
  • Refloating operations and grounding response
  • Dewatering, bunker removal and cargo removal support
  • Place-of-refuge coordination
  • Marine firefighting coordination through specialist partner networks
  • Response planning for LNG, methanol, ammonia and lithium-ion battery fire risk
  • Operational coordination in challenging regions and difficult operating environments
